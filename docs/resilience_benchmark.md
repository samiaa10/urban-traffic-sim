# Disruption and resilience: experiments

Generated by `python -m backend.simulation.benchmark_resilience --save` on arm64 / Python 3.9.6 (2026-11-18), 27 minutes in all. Seeds 1 to 5; each figure is the mean ± a 95% confidence interval over the five. See [RESILIENCE.md](RESILIENCE.md) for the model and what the measures mean.

Every scenario: osm traffic model, 1,500 trips leaving over 30 minutes, dynamic rerouting, 20 ambulance calls from 1 minute (one every 90 s), one central station with 3 ambulances unless stated. Disruptions start at 5 minutes and last 20 (the flood then recovers over 10). Busiest roads (from the normal run, seed 1): Grapes Hill, Grapes Hill, Chapelfield Road.
